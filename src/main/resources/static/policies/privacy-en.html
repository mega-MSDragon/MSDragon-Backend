Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-01 · Version: v1 · 한국어

Megajok (메가족, hereinafter "the Team") takes the privacy of users of Mosyeoyong (모셔용, hereinafter "the Service") seriously and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains why the Team collects and uses personal information and how it is protected.

1. Personal information we collect and how we collect it

The Team collects only the minimum information required to provide the Service.

CategoryItemsWhen collected

Beyond the items above, the Team does not collect sensitive information or unique identifiers such as resident registration numbers, bank or card details, or medical examination records. Information is collected only when a user enters it directly in the Service or when it is provided by the social sign-in provider.

2. Purposes of collection and use

  • Identifying members, signing in, and maintaining member accounts
  • Disconnecting the app from the social sign-in provider when an account is deleted
  • Connecting family members and sharing trip information within a family
  • Recommending destinations and courses suited to a parent's preferences and mobility
  • Optimizing travel routes and providing trip schedule guidance
  • Creating and signing the trip pledge and providing the signed document
  • Collecting post-trip feedback and generating the trip report
  • Providing travel guidance through the AI assistant
  • Providing nearby facility information at the user's request (only with optional consent)
  • Responding to errors, preventing misuse, and improving service quality

3. Consent items and the right to refuse

The Team separates required and optional consent and stores each decision together with the terms version.

  • Required — Consent to the collection and use of personal information. You may refuse, but sign-up and use of the Service are not possible without it.
  • Optional — Consent to location-based nearby facility guidance. You may refuse and still sign up and use most of the Service; only the nearby facility feature is unavailable.

4. Retention and use period

  • As a rule the Team retains personal information while you remain a member and destroys or anonymizes it without delay when you delete your account. At that point the social sign-in identifier is replaced with a value that cannot be re-identified, and the name, age range and gender are anonymized.
  • If you sign in again with the same social account after deletion, it is treated as a new sign-up and the previous member's trip records are not restored.
  • Information combined with other users' records, such as trips created together by family members, may remain in anonymized form so that those users' records are preserved.
  • Where applicable law requires a separate retention period, the information is kept for that period.
  • AI chat history is stored per trip to maintain conversation context and is destroyed or anonymized together with the account when it is deleted.
  • The social sign-in provider authentication token is deleted immediately after it is used to disconnect the app during account deletion.

5. Provision to third parties

The Team does not sell personal information or provide it to third parties for marketing purposes. It is provided only where there is a legal basis or where the user has given separate consent.

Because family connection is a core feature, the following information is shared among members connected as one family. This is inherent to the Service and takes place by consenting to the family connection.

  • Name (nickname), role and relationship label (such as Mom or Dad)
  • The parent travel profile and the derived travel personality type
  • The schedule, course, route and place notes of trips taken together
  • The trip pledge text and every participant's signature
  • Trip feedback results and the contents of the trip report

AI chat history is stored separately for each user and is not disclosed to other family members.

7. Handling of location data

  • Location data is collected only at the moment you request nearby facility guidance.
  • The collected coordinates are used solely to produce the results for that request and are not stored on the server.
  • Distances shown are straight-line distances on a map and may differ from actual walking or driving distance and travel time.
  • You may withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings.

8. Rights of users and legal representatives

  • You may view and correct your personal information at any time, and you may withdraw consent to processing by deleting your account within the Service.
  • Requests to access, correct, delete or suspend the processing of personal information may be made through the contact below, and the Team will act without delay.
  • The Team does not accept sign-ups from children under the age of 14.

9. Destruction of personal information

  • Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay.
  • Information held in electronic files is deleted by a method that prevents recovery or anonymized so that it cannot be re-identified; printed material is shredded or incinerated.
  • When an account is deleted, the Team asks the social sign-in provider to disconnect the app. This does not delete your Kakao account or Apple ID itself; it severs the link between that account and the Service so that the Team no longer has access.

10. Measures to secure personal information

  • Passwords are never stored; social sign-in authentication is used instead.
  • Tokens used to keep you signed in are stored as hashes and rotated periodically.
  • HTTPS encryption is applied to all communication.
  • The social sign-in provider authentication token is used only to disconnect the app and is never written to logs.
  • Access to systems that process personal information is limited to the minimum number of people whose work requires it.
